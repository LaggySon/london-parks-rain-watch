    Only numerical weather prediction models are used: ECMWF, the Met Office, DWD and NOAA, read through Open-Meteo. Consumer forecast sites were removed because their rainfall is published as vague bands and their “chance of rain” had to be invented. Rain input is each model’s own total across the days it actually covers, converted to a daily rate before weighting, so a short-range model is not penalised for stopping early. A model that publishes no probability is left out of the probability column rather than given a made-up one. Chance of a wet day is not the chance of green grass — the green figures come from a soil-water-balance and turf model driven by the past 92 days of weather and a 52-member rainfall ensemble. Per-park soil, root depth and irrigation coverage are documented assumptions, not measurements; these are estimates, not official forecasts.
